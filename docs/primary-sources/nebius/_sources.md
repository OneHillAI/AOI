## Re-read 2026-11-19 (freshness refresh)
**The governing contract has moved.** The standalone Token Factory Terms of Service now apply only to customers with an active reserve as of 2026-09-15; everyone else is governed from 2026-09-28 by the Nebius Services Agreement plus the Token Factory Supplemental Terms (effective 2026-10-22). The Speculative Decoding training clause survived the move (now Supplemental Terms 5.1(b) and 5.4), and the Quick Guide still tells readers the opposite in plain language.
