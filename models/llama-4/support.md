# Support - Meta Llama 4 (multimodal)

_Troubleshooting, versions, security disclosure, channels and deprecation._

<!-- item: troubleshooting -->
## Common problems & fixes

Recurring issues are multimodal input formatting, very-long-context memory pressure, MoE sharding
for Maverick and Behemoth, and chat-template or special-token errors. The list is not
exhaustive. The Llama-ecosystem community and cloud partners are the practical first stop.

<!-- item: release-versioning -->
## Versions, changelog & cadence

Scout and Maverick are released, with the larger Behemoth alongside, on the verified `meta-llama`
organisation with per-version licence tags. The Hugging Face revision hash is the changelog
anchor.

<!-- item: security-disclosure -->
## Security / vulnerability disclosure

Meta provides a reporting path (LlamaUseReport@meta.com) and ships Llama Guard and Prompt Guard
as a safety layer. A formal model-vulnerability-disclosure policy is limited.

<!-- item: channels -->
## Community & support channels

The `meta-llama` organisation, Meta's Llama site and docs, and the very large Llama-ecosystem
community and cloud partners.

<!-- item: deprecation -->
## Deprecation / end-of-life policy

No deprecation or end-of-life policy is published for Llama 4 checkpoints. Prior versions remain
downloadable, but no sunset commitment is documented.

<!-- item: known-issues -->
## Tracked known issues

The standing issues are the EU-domiciled multimodal licence gap, the systemic-risk status of the
large models, the 700M-MAU commercial trigger, and the download access gate. See Assess for the
detail.
