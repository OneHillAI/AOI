# Support - GLM-4-9B-Chat (original, glm-4 licence)

_Will it be maintained? Troubleshooting, versioning, security disclosure, channels, and
deprecation live here._

<!-- item: troubleshooting -->
## Common problems & fixes

The recurring issues are 1M-context memory blow-ups, older chat-template / tool-call
conventions that differ from the current GLM line, and quant-quality trade-offs. Not
exhaustive - the community around this widely-used 2024 model is the practical first stop.

<!-- item: release-versioning -->
## Versions, changelog & cadence

The original GLM-4-9B-Chat / -Chat-1M (and the glm-4-9b base) are on the `THUDM` organisation.
For new work they are **superseded by GLM-4-9B-0414 and the 4.5/4.6 line** (the `glm` entry).
The **Hugging Face revision hash is the changelog anchor**.

<!-- item: security-disclosure -->
## Security / vulnerability disclosure

A licence contact exists (license@zhipuai.cn), but no formal vulnerability-disclosure / security
policy was found for the weights. Factor this into your own incident-response plan.

<!-- item: channels -->
## Community & support channels

The `THUDM` Hugging Face organisation and GitHub, plus a community around this widely-used 2024
small model.

<!-- item: deprecation -->
## Deprecation / end-of-life policy

No published deprecation or end-of-life policy. The model is effectively superseded by the
current GLM line but remains downloadable, with no documented sunset commitment - recorded as a
gap.

<!-- item: known-issues -->
## Tracked known issues

The standing issues are the revocable, registration-gated licence, dated capability,
China-aligned alignment, and the absence of a first-party guard model. See Assess for the
detail.
