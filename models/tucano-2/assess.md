# Assess - Tucano 2 (Qwen-derived 0.5B, 1.5B and 3.7B; Base, Instruct and Think)

_Openness, licence, provenance, EU AI Act position, safety, evaluation and the AOI score._

<!-- item: intended-use -->
## Intended & out-of-scope use

Adopt the Instruct and Think models as Portuguese assistants only after your own safety evaluation, and the Base models as a starting point for fine-tuning. The cards give the primary use as research and development on Portuguese language modelling, with fine-tuning for deployment allowed under Apache-2.0 (`ev-card-instruct`, `ev-card-base`). The Base cards say the models are not for deployment or human-facing use, are not fine-tuned for downstream tasks and are for Portuguese only. The Think card describes a reasoning model with no coding training (`ev-card-think`).

<!-- item: limitations -->
## Known limitations, bias & failure modes

The cards list hallucination, social bias and toxicity from web data, weaker output in other languages, and repetition loops when the repetition penalty is low (`ev-card-instruct`).

The paper adds weaker instruction following and coding than Qwen3-4B. IFEval-PT is 41.67 against 79.33, and HumanEval is 47.56 against 86.59 for the 3.7B Instruct. It attributes the gap to the SFT data mix. Long-context retrieval is weak: RULER-PT at 4,096 tokens is 0.686 for the 3.7B Instruct against 0.979 for Qwen3-4B (`ev-paper`). A third-party run saw an invented fact about a Brazilian city and a wrong answer to who the model is (`ev-community`).

## Openness tier & components

The tier is `open_weights_recipe` (ceiling 4 on the openness dimension).

| Component | Grade | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Weights | open | safetensors for nine models, ungated, with Base intermediate checkpoints as branches (`ev-hf-platform`, `ev-card-base`) |
| Training data | partial | GigaVerbo-v2 (320B tokens), Synth (9.3B), SFT (4.1M examples) and Preferences (28,437 pairs) are downloadable; the Qwen3 base data is not published (`ev-gv2`, `ev-gv2-post`, `ev-qwen3-licence`) |
| Training code | open | llm-foundry, Apache-2.0, plus YAML configs and logs in each repo (`ev-code`, `ev-configs`) |
| Evaluation | open | a Portuguese lm-evaluation-harness fork (MIT) and published result files (`ev-code`, `ev-card-instruct`) |
| Documentation | open | model cards with limitations and a full paper (`ev-card-instruct`, `ev-paper`) |
| Licence | open | Apache-2.0 (`ev-licence-files`) |

The cards say that all data, code and recipes are open and reproducible. That holds for the Tucano stages. It does not extend to the Qwen3 stage underneath, and the pretraining corpus carries a mixed licence.

<!-- item: license -->
## Licence terms & permitted use

Apache-2.0 governs every Tucano 2 weights repo, ungated, with commercial use allowed (`ev-licence-files`). Eight of the nine Qwen-derived repos carry an identical LICENSE file. Tucano2-qwen-1.5B-Base has none and declares Apache-2.0 only in its card, so add the licence text yourself when you redistribute it. The file names four individuals as copyright holders.

The upstream models are Apache-2.0: the Qwen3 bases, and the Qwen2.5 7B, 14B and 32B models that wrote the synthetic data (`ev-qwen3-licence`, `ev-qwen25-licence`). An abliterated Qwen2.5-32B variant wrote the rejected harmful responses in the preference data and is also Apache-2.0.

The pretraining corpus is the open question. Its card says the combined dataset has no single unified licence and lists sources under ODC-By, CC0, CC-BY, CC-BY-SA and two non-commercial licences, CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0 (xlsum, 14 million tokens) and CC-BY-NC-4.0 (Bactrian-X, 8 million tokens) (`ev-gv2`). Neither the card nor the paper says whether those texts entered the sampled training mixture, and no document states what the NC terms mean for the weights.

<!-- item: provenance -->
## Supply-chain & provenance

The canonical source is `huggingface.co/Polygl0t`, which Hugging Face reports as not verified, with seven members (`ev-hf-platform`). Weights are safetensors only, each file has a Hugging Face LFS sha256, and the publisher ships no checksum file, signature or attestation. The API gives no malware-scan result.

Nine models derive from Qwen3 base models: Qwen3-0.6B-Base, 1.7B-Base and 4B-Base, with the Qwen tokenizer replaced by a 49K-token Portuguese tokenizer through Orthogonal Matching Pursuit transplantation (`ev-card-base`, `ev-paper`). They continue on 50B tokens (0.5B and 3.7B) or 100B tokens (1.5B), about 0.3% of the Qwen3 pretraining volume per the paper. The post-training data was generated by Qwen2.5 models. None of the nine is trained from scratch.

The only from-scratch release is Tucano2-0.6B-Base, a Llama-architecture 670M model trained on about 408B tokens, with no instruct variant (`ev-card-06b`). It is a separate follow-up entry.

<!-- item: eu-ai-act -->
## EU AI Act posture

The models are general-purpose AI. The Qwen3-4B base is about 8.6e23 FLOPs by OneHill's estimate from the Qwen3 card (6 x 4B parameters x 36T tokens), and Tucano adds 0.13% of that per the paper. Both are far under the 1e25 systemic-risk threshold, so no Article 55 duties apply (`ev-paper`, `ev-qwen3-licence`).

Apache-2.0 would normally qualify for the Article 53 open-source exemption, and no monetisation is documented. The exemption never covers a copyright policy or a public training-content summary. Neither exists as a standalone document. The paper and dataset cards list sources and licences for the Tucano stages, and the Qwen3 data is described only at the level of 36T tokens in 119 languages. The paper does not mention the Act, and no code-of-practice status appears in any document. This is guidance, not legal advice.

## Benchmarks & evaluation

All figures are the publisher's own, on a harness it released. Total scores are Normalised Performance Metric averages over the publisher's Portuguese suite (`ev-card-base`, `ev-card-instruct`, `ev-card-think`, `ev-paper`).

| Model | Result | Reference points in the cards |
|---|---|---|
| 3.7B Base, total | 59.21 | Qwen2.5-7B 57.97, Qwen3-4B-Base 57.86 |
| 3.7B Instruct, total | 53.64 (Knowledge and Reasoning 56.22, IFEval-PT 41.67, HumanEval 47.56) | Jurema-7B 53.03, Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct 51.71, Qwen3-4B 51.36 |
| 3.7B Instruct, single tasks | ENEM 72.92, BLUEX 64.53, OAB 54.31, Belebele 85.22, MMLU 64.64, GSM8K-PT 53.81 | Qwen3-4B MMLU 26.93 |
| 3.7B Think, total | 51.27 (Knowledge and Reasoning 54.07) | SmolLM3-3B 48.58, Qwen3-4B 46.35 |
| 1.5B Instruct, total | 37.54 | Qwen3-1.7B 36.30 |
| 0.5B Instruct, total | 26.08 | Qwen3-0.6B 22.21 |

One third-party run on 393 text-only ENEM questions gave 74.6% (95% confidence interval 70.0 to 78.6) for the 3.7B Instruct, ahead of Qwen3-8B in W4A16 at 67.9% (`ev-community`). The author warns that ENEM may be in training data. No independent study of the full suite exists in the sources found. The Qwen3-4B MMLU figure of 26.93 in the Instruct card is far below the 3B and 1.7B rows, which suggests a harness artefact. That reading is OneHill's inference, so treat comparisons on that task with care.

## Ownership verdict

Level: partial. Use and modify is strong, data control is strong, transparency is moderate, and reliability is weak because the safety dimension is rated 2. The `ownership` block in entry.yaml gives the verdict text and the conditions that would raise it.

## Contradictions and surprises

The paper and the Base cards call the whole suite fully open, reproducible and released under permissive licences. The GigaVerbo-v2 card says the corpus has no unified licence and lists two non-commercial sources, and the Qwen3 base data is not published. The accurate term for the suite is open weights with an open recipe.

The paper abstract gives sizes of 0.5 to 3.7B and the introduction says 0.5B to 3.5B. The cards report 490,799,104, 1,510,073,344 and 3,759,341,056 parameters.

Tucano2-qwen-1.5B-Base has no LICENSE file while its siblings have one. The Hugging Face organisation is unverified although the paper names its authors and their University of Bonn affiliations.
