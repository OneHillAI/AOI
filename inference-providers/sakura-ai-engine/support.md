# Support - Sakura Internet AI Engine

_How do we keep it running and get help? Status, security disclosure, channels and deprecation live here._

<!-- item: status-uptime -->
## Status page and uptime history

No AI Engine status page or uptime history was found. Sakura's cloud status page (status.cloud.sakura.ad.jp) lists Cloud
API per zone, websites and appliances only, and the manual says AI Engine is outside the SLA. The incident and maintenance
notice policy is written for Sakura Cloud resource types. The general maintenance and incident page renders its list with
scripts and could not be read. Expect no contractual uptime figure.

<!-- item: security-disclosure -->
## Security disclosure and trust centre

There is no trust-centre portal. Sakura's security page lists certifications and scopes, a check sheet (updated 2026-11-13)
answers a standard questionnaire, and a CSIRT (SAKURA.SIRT, founded 2015) exists. SOC 2 reports go to customers on request.
No vulnerability-disclosure policy, security.txt or bug bounty was found. The transparency report does not cover AI Engine.

<!-- item: channels -->
## Support and community channels

The manual routes AI Engine questions through the member-menu e-mail support, choosing "AI Engine" as the service. Sakura
Cloud standard support is free on weekdays 10:00 to 18:00 JST; paid premium support runs 24 hours with a 2-hour first reply
for faults. The pages do not say whether premium support covers AI Engine. Support, notices and contract are in Japanese,
with an English enquiry form for some topics only (basic terms article 4(3)). The manual is public on GitHub for issues.

<!-- item: deprecation -->
## Model deprecation policy

There is no per-model sunset schedule. Preview models "may be withdrawn without notice" and carry no stability guarantee;
prices may change when a preview ends. Sakura may change or end the free plan at its discretion (AI Engine terms article
2(3)) and may discontinue a service on one month's notice, or at once if authorities order it or a technology supplier
relationship ends (basic terms article 28). Terms changes need 7 days' notice by site posting or e-mail, and continued use
counts as acceptance (article 2(4)). The Private Edition page says you can keep using a specific model version (marketing, not in the terms).
