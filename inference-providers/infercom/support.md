# Support - Infercom

_How do we keep it running and get help? Security disclosure and support channels live here;
status/uptime and deprecation are documented gaps below._

<!-- item: security-disclosure -->
## Security disclosure & trust centre

Infercom publishes a **Trust Centre** (`infercom.ai/trust`) that documents zero data
retention, no-training, **ISO 27001**, the **CSA AI security assessment**, and GDPR / EU AI Act
alignment. This is an explicit, first-party trust surface - more than most newly launched
providers expose - and is the right starting point for a security review. A dedicated
coordinated-vulnerability-disclosure channel (e.g. `security.txt` / bug-bounty) was not
separately surfaced.

<!-- item: channels -->
## Support & community channels

Infercom is reachable **directly and via the Opper AI gateway**, alongside provider
documentation. The breadth of community channels (Discord/forum) and the response commitments
attached to paid tiers were not detailed in the sources reviewed, so confirm the support SLA
that comes with your plan during onboarding.
