# Support - Nebius (Token Factory)

_How do we keep it running and get help? Security disclosure, status/uptime, and support
channels live here; model deprecation is a documented gap below._

<!-- item: status-uptime -->
## Status page & uptime history

A public **status page** (status.nebius.com, independently browser-confirmed) shows a dedicated
**"Token Factory"** component as Operational, with a 90-day uptime-history view (no numeric %
shown on the face). Three brief, resolved incidents in the Sep 9-17 2026 window: partial metrics
unavailability in `eu-west1`, partial Object Storage 5xx errors in `us-central1`, and SkyPilot
cluster-creation errors (503) in `eu-north1`.

<!-- item: security-disclosure -->
## Security disclosure & trust centre

A published **Trust Center** (nebius.com/trust-center, read directly) documents **SOC 2 Type
II**, **ISO 27001/27701**, **CSA STAR Level 1**, and GDPR/CCPA posture. No public bug-bounty
programme was found.

<!-- item: channels -->
## Support & community channels

Product/console documentation (docs.tokenfactory.nebius.com and docs.nebius.com) is the primary
support surface. Support-tier response commitments were not detailed in the sources read this
pass - confirm what comes with your account tier during onboarding.

<!-- item: deprecation -->
## Model deprecation policy

No published model-deprecation or end-of-life policy was found on Nebius' public docs in the
sources read this pass. Because the catalogue is predominantly open-weight, a removed model can
generally be sourced from another provider - but confirm Nebius' own migration practice directly
if this matters for your workload.
