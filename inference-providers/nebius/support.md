# Support - Nebius (Token Factory)

_Status, security disclosure, support channels and model deprecation._

<!-- item: status-uptime -->
## Status page & uptime history

The public status page (status.nebius.com) shows "All Systems Operational" with a "Token Factory"
component under every region. It lists every product under every region, so it says nothing about
where Token Factory runs. A 90-day uptime bar carries no numeric percentage. Two Token Factory
incidents fall in its 14-day window, both resolved: 2026-11-05 (some models unavailable in
eu-north1, 10:30 to 11:07 UTC) and 2026-11-11 (Token Factory in tf-us2 unreachable from 07:33 to
07:41 UTC, cause under investigation).

<!-- item: security-disclosure -->
## Security disclosure & trust centre

The Trust Center (nebius.com/trust-center and its per-certificate pages) lists SOC 2 Type II with
HIPAA, a public SOC 3 report, ISO 27001/27701/42001 and others, CSA STAR Level 1 and a
security-team contact. No public bug-bounty programme is published.

<!-- item: channels -->
## Support & community channels

Product/console documentation (docs.tokenfactory.nebius.com and docs.nebius.com) is the primary
support surface. Support-tier response commitments are not stated, so confirm what comes with your
account tier during onboarding.

<!-- item: deprecation -->
## Model deprecation policy

Nebius publishes per-event notices instead of a standing policy. Serverless removals took place on
2026-06-22, 2026-08-31 and 2026-11-09, and one is announced for 2026-12-24. Each notice names a
replacement, says dedicated endpoints for the model are unaffected, and says requests are not
rerouted. No minimum notice period is stated, and the Legal Quick Guide says only that
deprecations "are announced in advance" by e-mail and in the platform.
