# Support - Groq

_Status, security disclosure, support channels and model deprecation._

<!-- item: security-disclosure -->
## Security disclosure & trust centre

Groq's trust centre (trust.groq.com) covers SOC 2 Type II, GDPR and HIPAA posture and a sub-processor list, but it is a JavaScript-only page and cannot be read. groq.com/security describes a private vulnerability disclosure programme through HackerOne. There is no public bug bounty.

<!-- item: channels -->
## Support & community channels

Support rests on product and console documentation (console.groq.com/docs). Groq does not publish the response commitments attached to each paid tier or the breadth of its community channels (Discord/forum), so confirm the support SLA for your tier during onboarding.

<!-- item: deprecation -->
## Model deprecation policy

Groq publishes a model-deprecation policy in its docs. Recent history shows frequent retirements (the Llama pair on 2026-08-16, Compound on 2026-09-21), with enterprise committed-spend contracts exempt.

Groq gives commercially reasonable prior notice before deprecating a model. Production models get a clear migration path and a recommended replacement, and preview models may be discontinued at short notice. If a customer has prepaid for a model that is deprecated without an alternative, Groq offers a pro-rata refund for the unused prepaid fees. Because the catalogue is open-weight, a removed model can generally be self-hosted or sourced from another provider, so deprecation is a migration task and not a lock-out.

## Status page & uptime history

Groq runs a public status page at groqstatus.com (incident.io; status.groq.com redirects to it). On 2026-11-19 it reported "fully operational", with August to November 2026 uptime of 100% for the API and the six production model components and 99.98% across seven preview components. No SLA or service credits are published, and the page's history URL returned 404.
