# Support - DeepInfra

_How do we keep it running and get help? Security disclosure and support channels live here;
status/uptime and deprecation are documented gaps below._

<!-- item: security-disclosure -->
## Security disclosure & trust centre

DeepInfra's **public trust centre** (trust.deepinfra.com/compliance) is the trust surface,
covering its ISO 27001 / SOC 2 Type 1 / GDPR posture. A **dedicated vulnerability-disclosure
process** - a published `security.txt`, advisory feed, or bug-bounty - was not surfaced,
so for coordinated disclosure plan to go through the trust-centre/support contacts
rather than a standing security channel.

<!-- item: channels -->
## Support & community channels

Support rests on **product documentation** (deepinfra.com/docs). The response commitments
attached to each paid tier and the breadth of community channels (Discord/forum) were **not
detailed** in the sources reviewed, so confirm the support SLA that comes with your tier
during onboarding.
