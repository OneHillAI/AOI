# Support - DeepInfra

_Status, security disclosure, support channels and model deprecation._

<!-- item: security-disclosure -->
## Security disclosure & trust centre

DeepInfra's trust centre (trust.deepinfra.com) carries compliance badges, control families, 46
policies and documents, a sub-processor list and an FAQ on retention, residency, the DPA and the
BAA, with a "Request access" button for restricted material. No `security.txt`, advisory feed or
bug-bounty programme is published, and the Terms (Sec 11(a)(v)) forbid probing or testing the
services without prior written consent, so use the trust centre or support contacts for
coordinated disclosure.

<!-- item: channels -->
## Support & community channels

Support rests on documentation at docs.deepinfra.com (with an `llms.txt` index), a Discord
community and a feedback email, all named on the status page, with enterprise contact through
sales. Response commitments per paid tier are not stated, so confirm them during onboarding.

<!-- item: status-uptime -->
## Status page & uptime history

status.deepinfra.com shows the current state, measured 90-day uptime per core service and per
model, a maintenance page and scheduled deprecations with named replacements; an RSS and API feed
are linked. It has no incident-history page, so past incidents and how they were communicated
cannot be reviewed, and there is no uptime SLA.
