# Support - DeepInfra

_How do we keep it running and get help? Security disclosure, status and support channels live
here; the model deprecation policy is a documented gap below._

<!-- item: security-disclosure -->
## Security disclosure & trust centre

DeepInfra's **trust centre** (trust.deepinfra.com) is now readable. It carries compliance badges,
control families, 46 policies and documents, a sub-processor list and an FAQ on retention,
residency, the DPA and the BAA, with a "Request access" button for restricted material. A
published `security.txt`, advisory feed or bug-bounty programme was not found, and the Terms (Sec
11(a)(v)) forbid probing or testing the services without prior written consent, so use the trust
centre or support contacts for coordinated disclosure.

<!-- item: channels -->
## Support & community channels

Support rests on **documentation** at docs.deepinfra.com (with an `llms.txt` index), a **Discord**
community and a feedback email, all named on the status page, with enterprise contact through
sales. Response commitments per paid tier were not found, so confirm them during onboarding.

<!-- item: status-uptime -->
## Status page & uptime history

**status.deepinfra.com** shows the current state, **measured 90-day uptime** per core service and
per model, a maintenance page and scheduled deprecations with named replacements; an RSS and API
feed are linked. It has no incident-history page, so past incidents and how they were communicated
could not be reviewed, and there is no uptime SLA.
