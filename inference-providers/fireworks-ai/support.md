# Support - Fireworks AI

_Status, security disclosure, support channels and model deprecation._

<!-- item: status-uptime -->
## Status page & uptime history

The public status page (status.fireworks.ai) reports "fully operational", with 90-day uptime of
100% on 22 of 23 tracked serverless endpoints and 99.50% on the lowest (Kimi K3.1 Pro), and no
listed incidents.

<!-- item: security-disclosure -->
## Security disclosure & trust centre

A trust centre exists at trust.fireworks.ai. Fireworks' blog, docs and DPA Schedule 2 say it
covers SOC 2 Type II, triple ISO certification and HIPAA compliance. The portal is JavaScript-only
and renders nothing without it, so no certificate, audit report or sub-processor list is verified.
No public vulnerability-disclosure programme (`security.txt` / bug bounty) is published.

<!-- item: channels -->
## Support & community channels

Product/console documentation (docs.fireworks.ai) is the primary support surface. Support-tier
response commitments are not stated, so confirm what comes with your account tier during
onboarding.

<!-- item: deprecation -->
## Model deprecation policy

No model-deprecation or end-of-life policy is published in Fireworks' public docs. Because the
catalogue is predominantly open-weight, a removed model can generally be self-hosted or sourced
from another provider. Confirm Fireworks' own migration practice directly if this matters for
your workload.
