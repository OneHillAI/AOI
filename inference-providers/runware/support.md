# Support - Runware

_How do we keep it running and get help? The trust surface and support channels live here._

<!-- item: security-disclosure -->
## Security disclosure & trust surface

The website is the trust surface: claimed **SOC 2 / ISO 27001** certification, encryption in
transit, no training on inputs, and default auto-purge of generated content. A dedicated trust
centre with downloadable reports, a `security.txt`, and a published vulnerability-disclosure or
bug-bounty channel are not published.

<!-- item: channels -->
## Support & community channels

Enterprise plans advertise a **shared support channel**, **24/7 on-call engineering** and priority
routing. The breadth of self-serve and community channels, and the standard-tier support
commitments, is not publicly detailed.
