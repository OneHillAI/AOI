# Support - Runware

_How do we keep it running and get help? The trust surface and support channels live here._

<!-- item: security-disclosure -->
## Security disclosure & trust surface

`runware.ai/trust` is now a Trust and Safety abuse-report form, and `trust.runware.ai` is a
JavaScript-only page that could not be read. The footer claims **SOC 2 / ISO 27001** certification with
no report behind it. A responsible-disclosure programme exists at `runware.ai/security-disclosure`
(no public bug bounty, safe harbour, 5-business-day acknowledgement target). Downloadable attestation
reports and a `security.txt` are not published.

<!-- item: channels -->
## Support & community channels

A help centre (`help.runware.ai`) and a docs site exist. Enterprise plans advertise a **shared support
channel**, **24/7 on-call engineering** and priority routing. Standard-tier support commitments are not
publicly detailed.

<!-- item: status-uptime -->
## Status page & uptime history

A public status page (`status.runware.ai`, incident.io) shows per-component uptime for August to
November 2026: API, Website, Dashboard and Playground at 100%, Models at 99.93%, Serverless at 99.90%.
It separates Official Models from Third-party Models. No SLA or service credits are published, and the
page's history URL returned 404 on 2026-11-19.
